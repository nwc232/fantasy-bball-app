Where do I find these?
  1. League ID is the leagueId= number in your league's URL on fantasy.espn.com.
  2. For a private league, log in to fantasy.espn.com on a computer, open DevTools → Application → Cookies → https://fantasy.espn.com, and copy the values of espn_s2 and SWID.
  3. The cookies go to your own server only and are kept in memory, never written to disk. If you tick "Remember", they are saved in this browser's local storage.
